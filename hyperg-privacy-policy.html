Thank you for using HyperG: FPS Meter & Game Space ("HyperG", "we", "our", or "the app"). This Privacy Policy explains how local hardware telemetry, permissions, and integrated third-party SDKs operate in accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google Play Data Safety Standards, COPPA, and GDPR-K.

1. Local Telemetry & HyperG Heuristic Engine

HyperG is built as an account-free system telemetry utility and Game Space overlay:

  • On-Device Telemetry: CPU, GPU, RAM, network throughput/ping, display refresh rate, and battery thermal metrics are read and computed locally on-device in volatile RAM and are not uploaded by HyperCore Labs.
  • Heuristic Estimation Disclaimer: On non-rooted devices or environments where Android OS / SELinux restricts direct access to certain hardware kernel nodes (such as GPU busy sysfs counters), some telemetry readings (such as GPU Est. Workload / ~XX% values) are predictions or estimates calculated locally by the HyperG Heuristic Engine from readable CPU utilization, core frequency scaling, and thermal signals rather than raw hardware counters. Readings are shown as N/A when underlying system sensors are unavailable.
  • Account-Free Use: HyperG requires no sign-in and does not collect your name, personal email address, phone number, contacts, biometrics, or precise GPS location.

2. System Permissions & Purpose

HyperG requests only the permissions strictly necessary for real-time HUD telemetry and session reporting:

  • Display Over Other Apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used solely to project the floating FPS HUD and Esports Crosshair Reticle over your games. It does not intercept user input or capture private screen content.
  • TrueFrame™ Screen Capture & Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Maintains the active telemetry overlay and processes on-device frame presentation intervals transiently in volatile RAM to measure rendered FPS.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays the active foreground HUD notification and session benchmark summary alerts while gaming.
  • Usage Access (android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Optional permission used strictly on-device to identify the active foreground game package during Game Space benchmark sessions.

3. TrueFrame™ & MediaProjection Privacy

When TrueFrame™ FPS measurement is active:

  • Local Calculation Only: Android MediaProjection is used exclusively on-device to measure frame presentation intervals and frame delivery timestamps.
  • Strict Privacy Guarantee: No video recording, audio capture, screenshot saving, disk storage, or network transmission of screen frames occurs.
  • Real-Time Discard: Frame buffers are processed transiently in volatile RAM and immediately discarded.

4. Advertising, Mediation & Analytics (Data Safety)

Consistent with HyperG's Google Play Data Safety declarations, integrated third-party Google and ad mediation SDKs process limited technical data:

  • Google AdMob & Mediation Partners (Unity Ads): Displays banner, app-open, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded video ads. These ad SDKs may process device or advertising identifiers (AD_ID / App Set ID), coarse IP-based location, ad interaction events, and diagnostic performance data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and your regional Google UMP (GDPR/UK) consent settings.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects crash traces, stability diagnostics, and aggregated feature telemetry events to detect issues and improve reliability across Android devices.
  • Free App Experience: Core HUD, TrueFrame™ telemetry, Device Specs, and Classic Gap Crosshair features are free, alongside optional opt-in rewarded video unlocks for extra themes and reticle customization, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

  • Local Storage Control: HyperG stores user preferences, theme selections, and session benchmark logs locally on your device without external user accounts.
  • User Deletion Mechanism: You can immediately erase all locally stored preferences and benchmark histories via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > HyperG > Storage > Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the app, and manage ad personalization via Android Ad Privacy settings or in-app Manage Ad Privacy (UMP).
